Who We Are

A farm built on patience, not shortcuts

Nestled in the heart of Kamuli District, Uganda, AVORA PIG FARMING is a family-owned enterprise raising healthy, free-roaming pigs the way we believe pigs should be raised — outdoors, unhurried, and well fed.

We pair traditional stockmanship — the kind passed down at feeding time, not read from a manual — with modern feeding and health protocols. Our farm spans over 10 acres of open pasture, where every animal has room to root, wallow, and grow at its own pace. It's slower than the shortcuts some farms take, and we think that's exactly why our herd is healthier for it.

Our mission: To give Ugandan farmers access to strong piglets, honest breeding stock, and the know-how to keep a herd thriving long after they leave our gate.

How We Got Here

Our Journey

2018

A single sty, two sows

AVORA started small — a family plot, two breeding sows, and a plan to do things properly from day one.

2020

Land, water, and room to grow

We expanded onto 10 acres of pasture and installed clean, reliable water for the herd year-round.

2022

Recognised across the district

Our breeding stock and piglet health protocols earned recognition from local agricultural bodies.

2026

200+ pigs, one standard

Today AVORA supplies piglets and breeding genetics to farmers across Kamuli and beyond — same standard as sow number one.
