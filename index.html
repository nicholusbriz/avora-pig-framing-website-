About Us

Our Passion. Your Success.

AVORA PIG FARMING is a modern pig farm located in Kamuli District, Uganda. We specialize in breeding and raising strong, disease-free pigs that give better growth and high returns.

Our goal is to support farmers and customers by providing quality pigs, reliable information and excellent customer service.
